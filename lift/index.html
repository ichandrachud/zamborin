Lift: drag the car, stop it level

You are the operator of a grand old hotel's lift. The car is heavy. Drag it in the shaft and it lags behind your hand; let go and it keeps going, braking over most of a floor from full speed. Land it level with a landing and the doors slide open and the light spills out. Land it between floors and it bumps, sags and sits there with the doors shut until you nudge it.

How to play

Everything you do is done to the car. There is no button to press for a passenger and no menu to open. You drive, you stop, and the building does the rest.

  • The verb: drag anywhere in the shaft to pull the car up or down. On a desktop you can hold the up and down arrow keys instead, which is kinder to a trackpad.
  • The feedback: let go and the car brakes. Level with a landing, the doors open and warm light lands on the floor. Between floors, a bump and no doors.
  • The goal: the number over someone's head is the floor they want. Carry them there and they tip you.
  • Restart: always free, and it costs you nothing but the tips on the counter.

Why it is clever

Almost every lift game is a puzzle about which button to press. This one is about weight. The brake needs nearly a whole floor to stop the car from full speed, so a good stop is a decision you make early, before you are anywhere near the landing you want. Landing one first time pays a small bonus, which is the only thing separating a careful operator from a careless one.

This is an early build. It is the car, the stop and the light. The people waiting do not yet run out of patience, so nothing can go wrong yet.

Tips

  • Let go a floor early. Trying to steer the car onto the landing is how you end up between floors.
  • A short hop needs a short pull. Full speed for one floor will always overshoot.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lift free? Yes, completely free to play, with no account or download, in any modern browser on phone, tablet or desktop.

Does it work on mobile? Yes. The shaft sits right of centre on a phone so your thumb never covers the landing you are serving.

How do I win? There is no losing yet in this build. Carry people to the floor they asked for and the tips go up.

Why did the doors not open? The car stopped between floors. Nudge it and let go again.
