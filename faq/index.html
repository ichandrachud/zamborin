Frequently asked questions

Are the games free?

Yes — completely free to play, with no account, download, or purchase required, ever. The site is kept free by a small number of ads.

Do I need to create an account or log in?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Open a game and play. We don't ask for your name, email, or any personal details.

Do the games work on my phone?

Yes. Every game is built to run on phones, tablets, and desktops in any modern browser. Each one lays itself out for the screen it finds, so a phone gets a portrait board sized to your handset rather than a shrunken copy of the desktop one.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Everything runs right in your browser using standard web technology. Nothing is downloaded or installed on your device, so there's nothing to update and nothing that can carry malware.

How does Zamborin make money if the games are free?

The site is supported by a small number of advertisements served through Google AdSense. Those ads cover our costs and keep every game free. See our Privacy Policy and Cookie Notice for how advertising works here.

Do you collect my data?

Very little. The games save a few settings (like your sound preference) on your own device, and we count visits with a privacy-friendly, cookieless tool. Advertising partners may use cookies as described in our Cookie Notice. We never ask you to hand over personal information.

Why do some visitors see a cookie banner?

Visitors from the EU, UK, and Switzerland get a consent banner the first time they visit, so they can accept or reject personalised advertising cookies. Everyone else sees ads without the prompt.

How do I get better at the games?

We write a strategy guide for each game. Browse them all in the Guides section.

Can I request a game or report a bug?

Please do. Send bug reports, game ideas, and feedback through the contact page — we read everything.
