Cookie Notice

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar technologies used on Zamborin Games (zamborin.com). We keep them to a minimum. For the fuller picture of how we handle data, see our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar technology that saves small bits of data on your device. Both let a site remember things between visits.

What we use, and why

Essential / functional storage. Our games save a few preferences and progress on your own device using local storage — for example, whether you've muted the sound, or a saved game state. This never leaves your device and isn't used to track you.

Advertising cookies. The site is supported by ads served through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and — where permitted — personalise ads based on your interests. These are set by Google, not by us.

Analytics. We use Vercel's privacy-friendly, cookieless traffic counting. It doesn't use cookies to identify you.

Consent (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

If you're visiting from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you'll see a consent banner the first time you arrive, letting you accept or reject personalised advertising cookies before any are set for that purpose. You can change your choice at any time by clearing the site's cookies in your browser and reloading the page.

How to control cookies

  • Manage or block cookies in your browser settings (every major browser lets you do this).
  • Control Google's personalised advertising at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising from many providers at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu.

Blocking cookies won't stop the games from working — they run entirely in your browser.
