About Zamborin Games

Zamborin is a small independent studio making fast, friendly games that run instantly in your browser, on a phone, a tablet or a laptop. There's nothing to download, no sign-up, and no waiting: every game is free, loads in about a second, and is easy to share with a link. No login, no accounts, just open a game and play.

What we make

Hand-crafted browser games that respect your time: quick to start, friendly on mobile and desktop, easy to share. Each game is built with vanilla HTML, JavaScript and Canvas, with no engines and no bloat. The whole catalogue is a single static site that loads fast on any connection.

  • Orbit — spin concentric rings until light threads out to every bulb
  • Bloom — rotate pipes to carry water from the tap to every flower
  • Untangle — drag the dots until no two edges cross
  • Tessera — an endless falling-letter word puzzle
  • Sluice — slide whole rows and columns of pipe until the water reaches
  • Fold — fold a creased sheet to bring a cut-up picture back together
  • Mobile — hang coloured shapes on a mobile until every arm sits level
  • Prism — steer light through a prism and fill every gem with its own colour
  • Zood — a bubble shooter; fire Zoods at the cluster and match three or more to pop them
  • Carrom — the South-Asian tabletop classic, three difficulty levels
  • Ludo — race four tokens home before three AI opponents

How we work

The studio runs lean. Every game is small enough to be built and shipped end-to-end by one or two people. We add new games regularly. When a game doesn’t work, we cut it. When one resonates, we expand it.

How we make money

The games are free and always will be. The site is supported by a small number of advertisements served via Google AdSense. Visitors from the EU, UK, and Switzerland get a consent banner the first time they visit; everyone else sees ads without the prompt. See our privacy policy for details.
