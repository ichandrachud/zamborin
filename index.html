About our free browser games

Every Zamborin game is hand-built as a small, self-contained web page — no game engine, no heavy downloads, no accounts to create. They're designed to be picked up in seconds and to feel good on both touchscreens and keyboards. We add new games regularly, keep the ones people enjoy, and quietly retire the ones that don't land. Here's a closer look at the current line-up:

Bloom — pipe-rotating garden puzzle

A calm irrigation puzzle. Water sits at a tap in the middle of the garden, and the ground is tangled with pipes turned the wrong way. Rotate them, one tap at a time, until the water threads out to every flower and opens the whole garden into bloom. Every board is solvable, the water shows your progress as it spreads, and the layouts grow more knotted as you climb.

Fold — origami folding puzzle

A quiet logic puzzle about paper. Each level is a creased sheet dotted with pairs of coloured marks: a solid dot and a matching ring. Tap a crease and the smaller flap folds over, carrying its marks with it, until every dot lands on its ring. Easy to start, satisfying to untangle, with your progress saved as you climb the levels.

Zood — bubble-shooter puzzle

Fire quirky little character tiles up at a hanging cluster and match three or more of a kind to pop them; any Zoods left dangling drop too. Bank shots off the walls, keep the cluster from reaching the line, and clear every last Zood.

Empyrean — free-flight aerial action

Fly a lone fighter over vivid, hand-painted worlds, steering your own heading as you dogfight enemy planes and bomb ground targets across city, ocean and desert missions. Choose your aircraft and bombs, then clear the skies.

Tessera — endless word puzzle

Part falling-block puzzle, part Scrabble. Guide letter tiles into a grid to spell words across and down; longer words score far more, and the tiles fall faster the longer you last. A quick, brainy high-score chase.

Untangle — daily logic puzzle

A tangle of dots and lines lands in front of you with every edge crossing another. Drag the dots until no lines cross. Every puzzle is solvable, and the difficulty climbs from a gentle tutorial to endless Master-level tangles.

Carrom — flick-and-pocket board game

A digital version of the South-Asian tabletop classic. Aim and flick the striker with a slingshot pull to pocket your discs before the computer clears theirs, across three difficulty levels. Bank shots and cut angles behave just like the real board.

Ludo — the classic dice race

Roll the die and race all four of your tokens home before your opponents. Land on a rival to send them back to the start, chain your sixes, and play safe or aggressive as the board demands — in 2-, 3- or 4-player games against the computer.

All of them are free, run in any modern browser, and work offline once loaded. Have a request or found a bug? Head to our contact page — we read everything.
