Bunny: the sliding brick puzzle

A rabbit, a carrot, and a wall of coloured bricks between them. Slide the bricks until there is a way through. The catch is that the same corridor which lets the rabbit reach its carrot will also let the fox reach the rabbit.

How to play

Drag any brick with studs on it and it slides one square. Anything without studs is scenery and will not move: a garden wall, an iceberg, a traffic cone.

  • Watch what walks. The rabbit paces as far as it can get and back, and so does every hunter. That pacing is not decoration, it is the answer to "what is connected to what", drawn for you continuously.
  • Open a way to the carrot. The moment the rabbit can walk to it, the level is won.
  • Never open a way to the hunter. The moment it can walk to the rabbit, it does.
  • The bomb. A brick carrying a bomb is destroyed when you move it, turning an obstacle into open ground. Some walls cannot be opened any other way.
  • Undo takes back one slide at a time, as far as you like.

Your progress saves automatically. There is no timer.

Why it is clever

Every one of these levels is decided by a single question: which patch of open ground is joined to which. The carrot in the rabbit's patch means you have won. A fox in it means you have lost. One rule, one verb, and no separate lose condition to learn, because opening a path is how you do both.

What makes it readable is that the game never asks you to work that out. The animals walk their own territory, so the boundaries are visible the whole time. Nothing is hidden behind a number, a legend, or a tutorial.

Tips

  • Watch the hunter before you touch anything. How far it paces tells you which side of the board is dangerous.
  • A brick slid straight out of a doorway lands in front of the doorway. To open a wall, slide its brick sideways along the wall instead.
  • If a wall has no room for its brick to slide aside, look for the bomb.
  • A move that seems safe can still shut the only route you had. Undo is free.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bunny free? Yes, completely free, with no account and no download, in any modern browser on phone, tablet or desktop.

Does it work on mobile? Yes. The board fills a phone screen and bricks are dragged with a thumb.

How do I win? Open a corridor from the rabbit to the carrot without opening one to a hunter.

Is every level solvable? Yes. Every level is checked by an exhaustive solver before it ships, and the shortest solution is known for each one.
